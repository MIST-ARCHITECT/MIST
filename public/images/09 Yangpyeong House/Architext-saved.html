Seojong Monolith: A Modern Steel-Frame Retreat / 서종 모놀리스: 현대적인 철골조 전원주택

Located in the serene landscape of Yangpyeong, this residence blends industrial steel framing with refined brickwork. The gabled silhouette creates a striking presence against the mountains, while the high-ceilinged interiors offer an airy, light-filled sanctuary for modern country living.
